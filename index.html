The "Digital Arrest" Police Impersonation Scam

Scammers pose as Cyber Crime Police or Customs officials over video calls, claiming illegal items were found under your identification number. They manipulate victims into staying on video for hours ("digital arrest") and transferring money for "verification".

HOW SCAMMERS TRICK YOU
1
Panic Phone Call

Caller claims your identity was used in international money laundering.

2
Fake Uniform & Police Backdrop

They demand you switch to a Skype/WhatsApp video call with a spoofed government seal.

3
Demands Security Deposit

They insist you wire funds to a "Supreme Court Escrow Account" for forensic clearing.

🛡️
DEFENSE RULE There is NO legal provision for "Digital Arrest". Real police and courts never conduct arrests or interrogations over WhatsApp/Skype or demand money transfers.
